# Design and Multiphysics Simulation of a Lightweight Ventilated Brake Rotor for Electric Vehicles

> An engineering design and CAE project focused on the preliminary design, thermal loading analysis, cooling model development, and transient thermal simulation of a lightweight ventilated brake rotor for electric vehicle applications using ANSYS Workbench.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Electric vehicles require braking systems capable of safely converting vehicle kinetic energy into thermal energy while maintaining acceptable operating temperatures and structural integrity.

This project investigates the engineering design and simulation workflow for a **lightweight ventilated brake rotor intended for an electric vehicle**.

The work follows a structured engineering process:

        ↓
